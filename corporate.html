Our Training Methodologies

We understand that one size does not fit all. Our training solutions are customized to fit the learning style, schedule, and technical requirements of your teams.

1. Fresher Induction Programs

Comprehensive bootcamp-style training designed to transform fresh campus hires into project-ready software engineers within 30 to 90 days.

2. Lateral Upskilling

Advanced, short-term workshops for experienced professionals looking to upgrade to newer tech stacks (e.g., migrating from Java to Full Stack or learning GenAI).

3. Project-Specific Niche Training

Highly specialized consulting and training for teams about to embark on complex projects involving rare or niche frameworks and architectures.

Delivery Modes

  • Instructor-Led Training (ILT): Traditional classroom training at your corporate premises or our specialized labs.
  • Virtual Instructor-Led Training (VILT): Live, interactive online sessions via Zoom, WebEx, or Microsoft Teams with virtual lab access.
  • Hybrid Learning: A blended approach combining self-paced e-learning modules with live mentoring sessions.
